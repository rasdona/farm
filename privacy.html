Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, mobile number, email address, location, and profile details, along with usage data needed to operate the platform securely.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide and improve our services, connect you with farmers, workers, and buyers, send job and marketplace notifications, and ensure platform safety.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Profile details you choose to publish are visible to other platform users as part of the service. We may share data with service providers only as needed to operate the platform.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted storage and secure authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@ekrishi.vercel.app.
